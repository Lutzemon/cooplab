# Roadmap

The next milestone is a complete two-player story playthrough with useful bug
notes. There is no public release date; these are work areas, not promises.

## Now

- Test the campaign with two people on separate Internet connections.
- Check scene transitions, player visibility, HUD and dialogue together.
- Fix story gates that leave the second player behind.

## Next

- Test death, respawn, reconnect and natural map travel across more missions.
- Check Windows/Linux combinations and the first-time launcher experience.
- Work through remaining cinematic and weapon-presentation issues.

## Before a public test

- Complete a broader campaign pass and document the remaining limitations.
- Decide which networking arrangements can be supported for public use.
- Prepare distributable builds and clear installation/recovery instructions.

Closed-test builds and infrastructure are not available through this repository.
