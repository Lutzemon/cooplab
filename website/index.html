01 / THE IDEA

Multiplayer campaign.

The goal is simple: play the story with friends, without managing a pile of mods by hand.

CoopLab has been taking shape through successive builds, hands-on playtests and bug fixes driven by player feedback. The work is in the details: keeping the party in the same scene, showing dialogue to everyone and getting everyone through the next door.

02 / ENEMY HEALTH SCALING

More pilots.
Tougher opposition.

Bring backup. Keep the fight.

Enemy health scales linearly with the number of players. With two players, eligible enemies have twice their normal health. Enemy Titans and damageable story enemies are included.

The aim is to keep shared fights worth fighting as the party grows. Enemy numbers and damage stay unchanged, so the original encounters can keep their shape.

Scaling is optional. Training targets, allies and protected scene actors keep their original health. Existing enemies are not healed or rescaled when a player joins or leaves. Closed tests currently focus on two players; larger groups still need verification.

05 / FIELD QUESTIONS

Before you ask.

Can I play it yet? +

Not yet — I know that is a bummer. Testing is currently limited to an existing group. A public test is planned, but there is no public build, download or open beta sign-up yet.

When will it be released? +

No date has been set yet. The next milestone is a broader campaign playthrough, followed by fixes for the problems it uncovers.

What about Windows and Linux? +

Native launchers exist for both. Linux gameplay uses UMU and Proton. Mixed-OS campaign play is still a test target, not a confirmed full-story pass.

Is there a public server? +

No public multiplayer service is offered. This website does not provide server access, invitations or connection settings. A relay-based joining flow is being developed to make Internet co-op easier. Public hosting plans will be announced with a future test.

How is the project being made? +

Development is AI-assisted, with substantial human direction, debugging and hands-on playtesting. I work through the builds with real players and use their feedback to shape the next revision. CoopLab builds on Northstar.Coop and Northstar.
