01 / THE IDEA

A second pilot.
Not a second job.

The goal is simple: play the story with a friend, without managing a pile of mods by hand.

CoopLab has been taking shape through successive builds, hands-on sessions and fixes driven by player feedback. The work is in the details: keeping both players in the same scene, showing dialogue to both, and getting everyone through the next door.

04 / FIELD QUESTIONS

Before you
drop in.

Can I play it yet?+

Not publicly. Testing is limited to the maintainer’s existing group. There is no public build, download or open beta sign-up.

When will it be released?+

No date has been set. The next milestone is a broader two-player campaign playthrough, followed by fixes for the problems it uncovers.

What about Windows and Linux?+

Native launchers exist for both. Linux gameplay uses UMU and Proton. Mixed-OS campaign play is still a test target, not a confirmed full-story pass.

Is there a public server?+

No public multiplayer service is offered. This website does not provide server access, invitations or connection settings.

How is the project being made?+

Development is AI-assisted, with substantial human direction, debugging and hands-on playtesting. It builds on Northstar.Coop and Northstar.
